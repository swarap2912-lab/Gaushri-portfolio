A gentler way forward

Rooted in reverence · Guided by care

Every cow deserves
a place to belong.

Gaushri Cow Welfare Foundation is devoted to a simple belief: every cow deserves safety, nourishment, and a life treated with dignity. We bring rescue, shelter, and everyday care together under one compassionate promise.

From urgent support for a cow in need to a peaceful, lasting home, we stand for thoughtful care that honors every life.

Compassion in action

From the first call for help to a safe place to rest, our promise is to show up with kindness, skill, and care that lasts.
